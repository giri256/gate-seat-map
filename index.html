Exact vs near degree

Exact: CCMT lists your exact degree (or "any B.E./B.Tech discipline") for that programme and paper.

Near: it lists a degree in the same branch family (say EEE when you have EE) but not yours. Institutes often treat these as equivalent, but CCMT matches the degree name on your certificate, so check before you lock a choice.

Groups and rounds

A programme can have several groups, each with its own merit list and set of GATE papers. Opening = highest score admitted in a round, closing = lowest. A dash means no seat in your category was filled that round.
